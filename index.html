Openclaw

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Openclaw is a personal automation tool run by one individual (Shahar) on his own home server. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.

What it does

With the owner's explicit consent, it connects to the owner's own Google account to:

  • Gmail: read and organize the owner's own email (for example, finding and archiving specific messages).
  • Google Calendar: read the owner's own calendars to send the owner reminders and trip briefings.

It is used only by the owner, only on the owner's own data, and only for the owner's personal benefit.
